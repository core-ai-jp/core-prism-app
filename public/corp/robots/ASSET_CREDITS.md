# CORE Sorter 01 — concept scene assets

- `sorter-01-v2.glb` and the concept JPEGs: original procedural geometry created for CORE Robots. The editable Blender source is reproduced by `scripts/robots/build_sorter_asset.py`. The scene is an explanatory concept, not manufacturer CAD, a physical simulation or evidence of an operating machine.
- `studio-small-09-1k.hdr`: **Studio Small 09**, Sergej Majboroda / Poly Haven, CC0. Source: https://polyhaven.com/a/studio_small_09 ; license: https://polyhaven.com/license . Used as a reflection and lighting environment.
- `draco/draco_decoder.wasm` and `draco/draco_wasm_wrapper.js`: Google Draco decoder, Apache License 2.0. The license is retained in `draco/LICENSE`. Runtime files are the versions distributed with the project's Three.js package.

## CORE humanoid appearance concept v2

- `core-humanoid-concept-v2.glb`, `humanoid-concept-v2.png` and `humanoid-concept-mobile-v2.png`: original procedural CORE geometry defined by `src/corporate/robots/createHumanoid.ts`, exported by `scripts/exportHumanoid.browser.ts`. The static views render the same geometry, materials and lighting as the interactive model.
- The owner selected the visual direction of a tall black-and-white humanoid with continuous curved surfaces and a full dark face. Tesla Optimus was a visual reference for proportions and material contrast (https://www.tesla.com/AI). No Tesla image, logo, mesh or CAD is included in these assets.
- Appearance concept only. These renders do not establish a built machine, mechanical feasibility, performance, safety certification or a relationship with any manufacturer.
- Earlier concept versions are retained.
